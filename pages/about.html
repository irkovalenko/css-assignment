We started Voltbook because buying a laptop was awful

Confusing model names, pushy add-ons and support queues that go nowhere. We thought a laptop shop could be calmer than that.

From a repair bench to a shop

Voltbook grew out of a small repair workshop. People kept asking the same question while they waited: "what should I have bought instead?"

So in 2021 we started stocking the handful of machines we actually recommended - the ones that came back for repairs the least.

How we choose what to sell

Every model on the shelf has to pass three checks: it has to be repairable, it has to hold its battery health past two years, and our own technicians have to be happy using it daily.

If a manufacturer glues the battery in or blocks part swaps, we don't stock it - no matter how well it sells elsewhere.
